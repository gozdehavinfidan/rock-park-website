The Rock Park way

No glass cases and no velvet ropes. Just tables of stone, open boxes and a scale, so you can see every piece before you buy it.

Buyers walk the aisles, pick their own stones and pay by weight. The more of a stone you take, the better the rate. Retail visitors are welcome at the same tables.

How wholesale works

Four steps, no fuss

No account to open. Bring your own boxes if you like, or use ours.

  1. 01

    Call or walk in

    Tell us what you're after, or just come and browse the aisles.

  2. 02

    Pick by tier

    Fill your boxes. Each stone's own weight sets its tier: Pick, Flat or Case.

  3. 03

    Weigh & pack

    Everything is weighed at the counter and packed for the road.

  4. 04

    Load up or ship

    Take it with you, or ask us about shipping larger orders.
